# Writing on GitHub

- **In English**, short and friendly. Thank first-time contributors.
- **Post on your own only what is factual**: a request for information, a duplicate, a reminder, a
  review of a bug fix, a request to rebase, a "fixed in" note.
- **Never on your own**: a refusal, closing a feature request, an answer about the roadmap. Those come
  to the owner first, on a card whose option carries the exact message.
- **Never promise a date or a release.**
- **A contributor's PR stays theirs.** When you finish it, push to their branch, and keep their
  commits and their name.
