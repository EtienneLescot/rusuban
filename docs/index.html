What happens while you're away

Rusuban runs on your machine. It acts where your rules allow, and seals in vermilion only what they keep for you. How it works

  1. 1

    Reads

    Every issue, PR, review and CI run of your repos

    Rusuban
  2. 2

    Judges

    Each item against the rules in its repo

    Claude Code
  3. 3

    Acts

    Comments, rebases, fixes in a worktree of its own

    Claude Code
  4. 4

    Merges

    Only behind locks, on the exact head it checked

    Rusuban's server
  5. 5

    Asks you

    Only what your rules keep for you, on a card

    for you

Rules are plain text in your repo

They live in .rusuban/rules/, on the default branch. Write orders and give every wait a duration. Without rules, Rusuban only watches.

錠

Agents never merge

They run on your machine, with your logins, on repos anyone can write to. So the server holds them with checks that do not depend on their obeying. It merges a PR alone only when all eight pass, on the head it checked.

One inbox, only your decisions

Every project in one inbox
A card says what the agents did and asks one question. Each option says what happens next.
Your desktop or your phone
Answer in the dashboard, or on your phone through your tailnet. Pings on ntfy, Slack, Discord, Teams or e-mail.
Rules that grow
When several cards ask the same thing, Rusuban proposes the rule that would answer them, as a PR you merge.
Every Claude conversation
The ones about an issue or a PR, linked to it, read and answered from the dashboard.
Tasks written as text
Run daily or on a click, in a project of their own when they concern no repo.
Test machines over SSH
Reserved one agent at a time. Your own desktop only after your yes.
